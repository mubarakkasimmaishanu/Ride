## 2. The Road Transport Union Strategic Alliance

By establishing mandatory registration with the Road Transport Union (NURTW / RTEAN) in Kaduna, Zaria, or Kano:
1. **Neutralizing the Union Threat**:
   - Rather than operating as an illegal pirate taxi service, platform drivers are recognized card-carrying members of the union.
   - Union officials at branch offices view the platform as a digital premium channel that modernizes inter-city transit.
2. **Safe Hub Buffer (100m Off-Park Rule)**:
   - Ensuring drivers pick up at designated safe hubs (university gates, filling station forecourts) avoids clogging park entrances and prevents street tout friction.
3. **The Automobile Mechanic & Dealer Supply Advantage**:
   - Mechanics and car dealers regularly travel between Kano's large automotive parts markets (Kofar Ruwa, Panteka), Zaria repair workshops, and Kaduna spray-painting ovens.
   - This provides constant, reliable car supply on all three corridor legs with clean, recently serviced vehicles.
