# Zaria–Kaduna–Kano Ride-Sharing Platform - Registration & Union Accreditation Skill

Used for governing user onboarding, vehicle registration, and **Road Transport Union Accreditation** across **Kaduna, Zaria, and Kano** for the ride-sharing platform.

---

## 1. Driver Eligibility & The Union Registration Mandate

To ensure complete legal compliance, community acceptance, and zero conflict with local transport authorities, **every driver must be officially registered with an accredited Road Transport Union branch in Kaduna, Zaria, or Kano**.

### The 3-Step Driver Accreditation Form

#### Step 1: Personal Identification & KYC
* Full Legal Name.
* Active Phone Number (WhatsApp-enabled).
* Live Camera Selfie / Headshot Photo.
* National Identification Number (NIN) or Driver's License Number.

#### Step 2: Road Transport Union Accreditation (Mandatory Gate)
* **Union Name**:
  * `NURTW` (National Union of Road Transport Workers).
  * `RTEAN` (Road Transport Employers' Association of Nigeria).
  * `State Transport Union` (Kaduna State Transport Authority / Kano State Transport Union).
* **Union Branch City**:
  * `Zaria` (e.g., Kwangila, Sabon Gari, or PZ Branch Unit).
  * `Kaduna` (e.g., Kawo, Central, or Mando Branch Unit).
  * `Kano` (e.g., Naibawa, Dawanau, or Kofar Ruwa Branch Unit).
* **Union Membership / Badge Number**.
* **Upload**: Photo of Union Membership Card OR Official Branch Registration Dues Slip.

#### Step 3: Vehicle Registration
* Vehicle Make (Toyota, Honda, Peugeot, Mercedes, Volkswagen).
* Vehicle Model (Corolla, Camry, Golf, Matrix, 406).
* Exterior Color & Plate Number.
* Passenger Capacity (Strictly 3 or 4 passenger seats max; overloading is prohibited).
* Exterior and Interior Photos (showing vehicle cleanliness and air conditioning).

---

## 2. Administrator Verification Checklist

Before an administrator marks a driver as `'approved'`, they must complete this 4-point review:

1. [ ] **NIN / License Validity**: Does the driver's name match their national identification?
2. [ ] **Union Registration Verification**:
   - Is the union membership card or branch registration slip legitimate and unexpired?
   - Is the union branch located in **Kaduna, Zaria, or Kano**?
3. [ ] **Plate Number Match**: Does the plate number on the vehicle photo match the registered profile?
4. [ ] **Passenger Comfort Standards**: Does the interior photo confirm private, clean seating (max 3–4 passengers)?

## 3. Passenger Onboarding (Zero Friction)

Passengers register in under 30 seconds:
* Full Name.
* Active Phone Number (masked for Nigerian formats: `080X XXX XXXX`).
* Password.
* Instant access to search trips across all three cities (Zaria, Kaduna, Kano) without delay.
